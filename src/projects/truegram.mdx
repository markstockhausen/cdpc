## The Problem

Social media platforms today are built on business models that monetize attention. To maximize profit, interfaces utilize manipulative designs—like infinite scrolling and variable rewards—to keep users engaged as long as possible[cite: 42, 98]. This creates a toxic cycle, particularly for young adults, where users fall into mindless scrolling patterns during idle moments, leading to anxiety, reduced focus, and a distorted sense of reality[cite: 94, 95].

## The Goal

Our goal is to create a **healthier social media feed** that respects the user's time and mental state. We aim to shift the paradigm from "time spent" to "quality of time," empowering users to connect with friends and find inspiration without getting trapped in engagement loops[cite: 142].

## Solution Overview

TrueGram redefines the social media architecture through four core pillars of conscious design:

    items={[
        { title: "Intent-Based Feed", body: "Disrupts the automatic habit of opening apps by asking 'What do you hope to achieve?' (e.g., Catch Up, Find Inspiration) before entering the feed[cite: 233]." },
        { title: "No Endless Scroll", body: "Eliminates the 'bottomless bowl' effect. Each session has a fixed number of posts based on a Coin Budget System, providing a clear stopping cue[cite: 288]." },
        { title: "Notification Bundles", body: "Replaces anxiety-inducing real-time pings with scheduled batches, allowing users to stay connected without constant interruption[cite: 248]." },

TrueGram is built on "Ethical Defaults": no advertising, transparent privacy, and no manipulative patterns. Our "Double Diamond Algorithm" prioritizes relationships over engagement, focusing on discovering friends and deepening connections rather than serving viral content[cite: 286, 290].

## The Designed Experience

TrueGram was created for **Young Adults (16-30)** and **Neurodivergent Users** who feel overwhelmed by mainstream platforms[cite: 149, 152]. We consciously exclude influencers and advertisers to protect the community from high-pressure engagement tactics.
